## 1. Executive Summary

In **Phase 7: Tracking History & Insights**, a comprehensive, offline-first historical analytics and insights engine was implemented for Reel Monster. Users can explore their previous Reel and Short consumption patterns across customizable calendar date ranges, inspect daily platform distributions, view an interactive activity chart, drill down into individual recorded timestamps, and review factual, non-judgmental behavioral insights.

All data is queried directly from local SQLite storage without external dependencies, network access, or cloud accounts.

### Key Deliverables Completed:
1. **Dedicated Tracking History Screen (`HistoryScreen.kt`):**
   - Accessible via the existing bottom navigation bar (`Screen.History`).
   - Clean Stitch-inspired Material 3 design with mint-green visual tokens (`#76D7A5`, `#247A58`, `#EDFAF4`).
   - Responsive, vertically scrollable layout containing date range pills, summary stat cards, platform breakdown bar, interactive activity volume chart, selected day detail card with timestamped event feed, and factual insights.
2. **Date Range Engine (`HistoryDateRange.kt` & `DateTimeUtils.kt`):**
   - Supports 5 distinct calendar periods:
     - **Today (`TODAY`):** Exact current calendar date.
     - **Last 7 Days (`LAST_7_DAYS`):** Past 7 calendar days inclusive [Today - 6, ..., Today].
     - **Last 30 Days (`LAST_30_DAYS`):** Past 30 calendar days inclusive [Today - 29, ..., Today].
     - **This Month (`THIS_MONTH`):** From Day 1 of the current month up to today (excludes future dates).
     - **Previous Month (`PREVIOUS_MONTH`):** Exact full prior calendar month [Day 1 to Last Day].
   - Handles month boundaries, year boundaries (e.g. Dec $\leftrightarrow$ Jan rollover), and leap years.
   - Populates continuous zero-activity calendar days without duplicate or missing dates.
3. **Historical Statistics & Attribution:**
   - Guaranteed consistency: $\text{Total Reels} = \text{Instagram Reels} + \text{YouTube Shorts}$.
   - Formula: $\text{Estimated Time Lost} = \text{Total Reels} \times 1.5\text{ minutes}$. Labeled clearly as **Estimated Time Lost** (never claimed as measured screen time).
   - Daily Average: $\text{Total Reels} / \text{Total Calendar Days in Range}$.
   - Active Day Pace: $\text{Total Reels} / \text{Active Days Count}$.
   - Peak Day: Highest-activity day identification with date and count.
   - Lowest Active Day: Identifies lowest non-zero activity day safely.
4. **Historical Activity Chart (`HistoricalActivityChart.kt`):**
   - Interactive segmented stacked bars distinguishing Instagram Reels (`#8B5CF6`) and YouTube Shorts (`#EF4444`).
   - Zero-activity days marked with subtle baseline stubs (`#E5E7EB`).
   - Horizontal scrolling support for 30-day and monthly ranges with auto-scroll to the most recent day.
   - Tapping any bar selects that day for in-depth inspection and highlights the bar with a count badge and accent border.
5. **Daily Detail Inspection & Individual Event Log:**
   - Selecting any day reveals full details: formatted calendar date, total reels, IG count, YT count, estimated time lost.
   - Lists real individual timestamped entries from `reel_events` table (e.g. `10:45 AM · 📷 Instagram Reel · +1 Chomp`).
   - Safe zero state if no events exist for the selected day.
6. **Objective Insights Section:**
   - Factual observations generated by `HistoryAnalyticsUseCase`:
     - Peak activity observation with date, total reels, and platform split.
     - Active day scrolling pace and frequency.
     - Instagram vs YouTube percentage split.
     - Trend comparison with the equivalent previous period (e.g. vs Yesterday, vs Previous 7 Days, vs Previous 30 Days, vs Previous Month). Handles 0 previous reels safely without misleading infinite percentages or divide-by-zero crashes.
7. **Clean Data Architecture:**
   - Single source of truth in SQLite (`daily_aggregates` and `reel_events` tables).
   - Reactive Kotlin `StateFlow` updates in `ReelRepository`.
